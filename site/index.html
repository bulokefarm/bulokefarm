The process

How ordering works

  1. Choose your pack

    Select the pack size that suits you and tell us whether you’ll pick up or want it delivered.

  2. Secure it with a deposit

    We require a deposit to secure your order. There’s no payment online — we email you an invoice for the deposit with payment details.

  3. We pack your order

    When packing we’ll confirm weights and get the packs ready for pick-up or delivery. Carcass weights can vary however we aim to get this accurate for each and every order.

  4. Pay the balance

    Once butchered and packed we will invoice you with the balance remaining. Our price is based on retail weight, meaning the weight of the product as it is sold.
